Privacy notice.

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who I am

This site is run by Sinead Murphy, trading as SM Marketing Group, Dublin, Ireland. I am the controller of the personal data described here. You can contact me at sinead@smmarketinggroup.com.

What I collect, and why

  • The contact form, call bookings and email enquiries. Your name, company, email address, phone number if you give it, and your message. I use them to reply and to work out whether I can help. The basis is my legitimate interest in answering business enquiries, and steps you ask me to take before a contract.
  • The checklist download form. Your name, email address, brand and where you are with a shopping centre. I use them to give you the checklist and to understand who downloads it. If you tick the box, I may also send occasional follow ups. The basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Client work. Information about your business and your team, and the records needed to deliver and invoice the work. The basis is the contract with you.
  • Customer interviews for a Diagnostic. When I speak to your customers, I tell them who I am, why I am asking and how their words will be used. I use their comments in reports without names unless they agree otherwise. The basis is my client's and my legitimate interest in understanding buyers, and I stop if someone asks me to.

Who else handles your data

  • Formspree processes form submissions for me. It is based in the United States, so data sent through the forms may be transferred outside the European Economic Area.
  • GitHub hosts this website. It receives technical information such as your IP address when you load a page.
  • Google Analytics measures how visitors use this site, but only if you accept analytics cookies. It receives your IP address, the pages you view and how you arrived. Google may process this data in the United States. The basis is your consent, which you can change at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.
  • Google Fonts. Your browser requests fonts from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.
  • Calendly handles call bookings. If you book a call, it collects your name, email and the time you choose.
  • My email and calendar providers hold messages and bookings.

I do not sell personal data. Analytics cookies only run if you accept them in the cookie banner, and I do not use advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 24 months.
  • Checklist downloads: until you unsubscribe, or 24 months after the last contact.
  • Client records and invoices: six years after the work ends, which Irish tax rules require.
  • Customer interview notes: deleted within 12 months of the final report, unless the client and I agree otherwise.

Your rights

You can ask me to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or stop using it, or move it to another provider. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email me and I will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Changes

If I change how I use personal data, I will update this page and the date at the top.
