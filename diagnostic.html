Where sales stall

Sales stall in one of three places.

Most founders spend on the first. In my experience the leak is more often the second. I find out which before you spend another euro.

  • Seen. Do the right buyers know you exist? I look at where your last 20 customers came from, how you show up in search and in AI answers, and who refers you.
  • Sure. Do they believe enough to decide? I look at your message, your proof, your pricing and what nearly stopped your last ten buyers.
  • Sold. Does your process let them buy? I look at how fast you reply, how you follow up, and whether you can see which source wins.

Real conversations, not a template audit.
