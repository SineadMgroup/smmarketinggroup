Working together

Three ways to work with me, scaled to your business.

01

Counsel

For a founder who has someone doing the work, such as a junior, an agency or a family member, and needs a senior head on it.

  • One 90 minute session a month
  • A 90 day priority plan, refreshed each quarter
  • Message access, with a reply within two working days
  • Not included: execution or supplier management

Minimum 3 months  |  30 days notice  |  Fee agreed on a call

02

Lead

For a founder led business with no marketing lead. I act as your head of marketing.

  • Ownership of the marketing plan and budget
  • A fortnightly session
  • Direction of up to two suppliers, or one junior
  • A quarterly review of your sales process
  • Not included: writing, design, posting, ad buying, media spend or sending outreach

Minimum 3 months  |  60 days notice  |  Fee agreed on a call

03

Growth

For a business with a small team or several suppliers, and a growth event ahead: a new market, a new site, funding or a sale.

  • Everything in Lead
  • A weekly touchpoint
  • Sales and marketing pipeline owned jointly with the founder
  • Investor reporting
  • Hiring your first in house marketer
  • Not included: volume execution, media spend or PR retainers

Minimum 6 months  |  60 days notice  |  Fee agreed on a call

Questions

Questions founders ask first.

What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing leader who works with your business part time. You get the leadership of a chief marketing officer without the cost of a full time hire.

How does working together start?

Most clients start with the Diagnostic, a fixed fee piece of work that finds where your sales are stalling and ends with a written report and a 90 day plan. The fee is agreed on a call before you commit.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Founder led brands in Ireland, mostly those that trade in person, such as shops, venues, clinics and stockists. Founders who sell online are welcome too.

How much does it cost?

Fees are agreed on a call, before you commit to anything. The Diagnostic is a fixed fee, and the monthly retainers depend on the level of support you need.
