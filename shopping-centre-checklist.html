Inside the checklist

Seven things to ask your shopping centre for.

Most centres have more marketing support than their tenants use. What you get depends on what you ask for, and when.

01

Launch support

Opening campaign, social and press coverage, a listing on the centre website and in centre screens.

02

The marketing budget

How the centre's marketing contribution is spent, the campaign calendar for the year, and a place in seasonal campaigns.

03

Prime exposure

Digital screens, entrance and mall space, pop up spots, window and wall placements.

04

Events and promotions

Christmas, back to school and local events. Ask three to six months ahead.

05

Your data

Footfall and shopper profile for your zone of the centre, ideally every month. This is the one very few brands ask for.

06

Introductions

Centre PR, local media, other tenants, and gift card or loyalty schemes.

07

Lease and signage terms

Marketing clauses, signage and trading obligations. Take legal advice before you negotiate.

A centre knows who walks past your door: footfall by day and hour, how long people stay, who they are. In ten years, very few brands asked me for it.

How to ask. Go to the centre's marketing manager, not only the leasing contact. Ask six to eight weeks before a launch. Bring a one page plan of what you offer in return: content, offers, events, customer stories.
