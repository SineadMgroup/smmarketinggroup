About Sinead Murphy

I ran marketing at Liffey Valley for ten years. Now I work with founders.

I have spent twenty years in marketing for Irish consumer brands, across retail, aviation and telecoms. For the last ten I was Marketing Director at Liffey Valley Shopping Centre. Ten million people a year came through the doors on my watch. In 2026 I started SM Marketing Group to work with founder led brands.

What the landlord's side taught me
Most founders never see how a centre decides which brands to back.

For ten years I sat on the landlord's side of the table. I saw which brands a centre backed, which ones struggled after opening, and why.

Now I use that for founder led brands. I work out where your buyers are stalling, in their own words. Then I either lead the fix with you, or leave you a plan your team can run.

You work with me directly. I am not an agency, and there is no junior between us.

Career record

Where the experience comes from.

01

Liffey Valley Shopping Centre

Marketing Director for ten years, working with the team at Hines. During that time the centre reached 10M+ visitors a year, footfall grew 20%, the ABC1 share rose from 62% to 71%, the average visit lengthened from 87 to 98 minutes and average spend per visit rose from €88 to €119. I was part of the team that secured Ireland's first concept Zara (35,190 sq ft) and built a pop up programme for emerging Irish brands. The centre won a Hines European ESG Award and a BREEAM Outstanding rating.
