Where sales stall

Sales stall in one of three places.

Most founders spend on the first. In my experience the leak is more often the second. I find out which before you spend another euro.

01

Seen

Do the right buyers know you exist? I look at where your last 20 customers came from, how you show up in search and in AI answers, and who refers you.

02

Sure

Do they believe enough to decide? I look at your message, your proof, your pricing and what nearly stopped your last ten buyers.

03

Sold

Does your process let them buy? I look at how fast you reply, how you follow up, and whether you can see which source wins.
